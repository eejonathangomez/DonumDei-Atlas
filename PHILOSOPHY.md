## Atlas philosophy

### Knowledge is institutional

Engineering wisdom must survive individual engineers, AI sessions, and project transitions. Atlas exists so understanding accumulates across the ecosystem rather than resetting with each new contributor.

### Experience becomes knowledge

Repository 0 defines how Donum Dei engineers. The Trading Platform produces engineering experience through building, investigating, and certifying. Atlas transforms that experience into discoverable institutional knowledge.

### Traceability is non-negotiable

Knowledge shall always remain traceable to its original source. Atlas preserves relationships as carefully as facts. A fact without provenance is incomplete.

### Relationships are knowledge

Facts alone are insufficient. Atlas preserves how concepts connect across domains — engineering to markets, software to mathematics, philosophy to practice.

### Truth-centered preservation

Atlas does not archive opinion as fact. Inherited constitutional discipline applies: truth before convenience, evidence before opinion, humility before certainty.
