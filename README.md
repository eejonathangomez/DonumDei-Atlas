## Mission

> Preserve knowledge.  
> Connect knowledge.  
> Make knowledge discoverable.  
> Preserve engineering wisdom for future generations.

Atlas is the **Knowledge Preservation Repository** of the Donum Dei Engineering Ecosystem.

Atlas is not a product. Atlas is institutional memory — so future engineers, AI assistants, and Donum Dei projects begin with accumulated wisdom rather than starting from zero.

## Ecosystem relationship

```text
EngineeringBridge (Coordination Authority)
        ↓ navigates the ecosystem
DonumDei-TradingPlatform (Constitutional — Trading Platform + platform engineering standards)
        ↓ produces experience; owns trading standards
Atlas (Constitutional — Institutional knowledge)
        ↓ preserves knowledge & experience
Academy (Constitutional — Education)
        ↓ owns curriculum / KR / KDI
```

Atlas **inherits by reference**. Atlas **does not duplicate** constitutional masters.

## Repository rules

Atlas shall **not**:

- Duplicate constitutional documents
- Redefine DonumDei-TradingPlatform engineering standards
- Replace EngineeringBridge coordination artifacts
- Store production code
- Become a product repository or AI application product

Atlas is the **knowledge layer**.
