# Strategic Map — Founder / Architect

Strategic frame for the Founder/Architect system. Permanent principles live here; weekly engineering state does not.

Entry: [START-HERE.md](./START-HERE.md) · Roles: [FOUNDER-ARCHITECT.md](./FOUNDER-ARCHITECT.md)

## North Star

Intelligence, providers, and tooling may change.

What must remain durable:

- Founder/Architect partnership
- Institutional knowledge and IP
- Architecture and operating principles
- Strategic intent and boundaries
- Ability to recover and continue

Donum Dei is the current product (“bread”). Future products may join the portfolio. The oven exists to bake bread — not to admire itself.

---

## The oven is not the bread

| | Meaning |
|--|---------|
| **Bread** | Products and customer value. Donum Dei is current bread; future products are bread. |
| **Oven** | Founder + Architect + durable knowledge + DORY + Cursor + GitHub + reusable skills + supporting tools (e.g. Jira/Confluence when justified) |

**Improve the oven only when it materially improves our ability to produce bread.**

### Preferred loop

Build oven → make bread → learn → minimally improve oven → make better bread → repeat.

### Anti-pattern

Build oven → improve oven → redesign oven → admire oven → never open bakery.

### Permanent strategic question

Does this advance the highest-priority Founder objective, or are we merely improving something because it is in front of us?

## Long-term goals

- Preserve Founder/Architect continuity across computers and AI providers.
- Keep knowledge provider-independent (Git + Markdown canonical).
- Ship and improve real products (bread) with healthy execution loops.
- Abstract reusable skills from proven work — not premature generalization.
- Add publishing/backup/tracking surfaces only when additive and non-duplicative.
- Maintain clean employer/IP provenance forever.

## Continuity as strategy — not as excuse

Continuity and resilience are a **strategic requirement** (see [FOUNDER-ARCHITECT.md](./FOUNDER-ARCHITECT.md) Founder Continuity Standard).

They are **not** a reason to stop bread development or to build a second operating system. Document the requirement; prove recovery later with a drill; keep shipping product.
